# Mailtrap Setup for Email Verification

This guide explains how to configure Mailtrap for email verification in your FastAPI backend.

---

## 1. Sign Up for Mailtrap

- Go to [Mailtrap](https://mailtrap.io/) and create a free account.

---

## 2. Get Your SMTP Credentials

- In Mailtrap, go to your Inbox.
- Click **SMTP Settings**.
- Copy the following values:
  - Username
  - Password
  - Host (usually `sandbox.smtp.mailtrap.io`)
  - Port (usually `587`)

---

## 3. Update Your `.env` File

Add or update these lines in your `.env` file:

```env
MAIL_USERNAME=your_mailtrap_username
MAIL_PASSWORD=your_mailtrap_password
MAIL_FROM=your@email.com
MAIL_PORT=587
MAIL_SERVER=sandbox.smtp.mailtrap.io
MAIL_TLS=True
MAIL_SSL=False
MAIL_HOST=smtp.mailtrap.io
```

Replace `your_mailtrap_username` and `your_mailtrap_password` with your actual Mailtrap credentials.

---

## 4. How It Works

- When a user registers, the backend sends a verification email using Mailtrap SMTP.
- The email will appear in your Mailtrap inbox for testing.
- No real emails are sent to users—Mailtrap is for development/testing only.

---

## 5. Troubleshooting

- If you do not see emails in Mailtrap, double-check your credentials in `.env`.
- Make sure your backend is restarted after changing `.env`.
- Check your backend logs for any SMTP errors.

---

**You are now ready to use Mailtrap for email
